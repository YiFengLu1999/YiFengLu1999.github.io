The first two entries paraphrase questions or conjectures attributed to the talk in the public accounts below. The third is our formulation of a limitation discussed there. None is presented as a verbatim quotation; video timestamps have not been verified. Every entry links to the corresponding passage in our published note.

“Open” refers to the specific remaining question stated in each entry, not an assertion that the entire field has made no progress. Research updates are concise readings of the linked paper abstracts, checked on {{ notebook.checked }}, not an exhaustive literature review. They are our updates, not claims that Ilya recommended those papers. New evidence may narrow, reformulate or resolve an entry.

“开放”指每项中具体写出的待解问题，不表示整个领域毫无进展。研究更新基于链接论文摘要的简要核对（{{ notebook.checked }}），不是穷尽文献的综述；这些补充也不代表伊利亚推荐过对应论文。后续证据可以缩小、改写或解决这些问题。
